AI Client Communication & Lead Qualification

A two-part Agentic AI capstone built in n8n: one workflow drafts compliant investment-client responses for human approval, while a Telegram bot captures and qualifies new leads with Supabase RAG.

View Workflows Sample Prompts Back To Project Card

Solution Overview

The capstone connects front-office communication with lead acquisition. AI is used for reasoning, summarization, and drafting, while n8n keeps the operational controls explicit: approval, logging, scoring thresholds, and notifications.

Customer Rapport Assistant

  • Captures client name, portfolio type, horizon, and investment concern from an n8n form.
  • Identifies concern category and emotional tone.
  • Drafts a compliant, non-advisory response for Gmail approval.
  • Logs approved drafts and manual review cases into separate Google Sheets records.

Telegram Lead Qualification Bot

  • Uses a Telegram bot as the public lead-capture entry point.
  • Collects name and email before normal conversation.
  • Answers service questions using a Supabase RAG knowledge base.
  • Scores lead intent from 0 to 100 and alerts sales when score is 70 or higher.

Governance Controls

Human Approval

Client-facing investment responses are routed through Gmail approval before they are treated as approved records.

Structured Output

Both agents return JSON so n8n can parse, validate, route, log, and recover from malformed output.

Public Sanitization

The published workflows remove tokens, private IDs, webhook metadata, and account-specific credentials.
