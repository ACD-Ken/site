What This Proves

The active Telegram lead qualification workflow is published in n8n and connected end to end across intake, RAG retrieval, scoring, Gmail alerts, and Telegram replies.

  • Telegram and webhook entry points feed the same normalized message path.
  • Supabase RAG and OpenAI support the AI lead qualification agent.
  • Qualified leads route to Gmail only after the sales handoff gate confirms complete lead details and buying/contact intent.

Solution Overview

The capstone connects lead acquisition with service knowledge. AI is used for qualification, RAG answers, and concise follow-up, while n8n keeps the operational controls explicit: registration, scoring thresholds, alerts, and knowledge ingestion.

Telegram Lead Qualification Bot

  • Uses `@AlsoCan_Bot` as the public Telegram entry point.
  • Captures name, email, use case, budget, and timeline before routing a lead.
  • Answers service questions using a Supabase RAG knowledge base.
  • Scores lead intent from 0 to 100, then alerts sales only when the score, contact details, and handoff intent are all valid.

Sales Knowledge RAG Ingestion

  • Accepts public-safe service catalog rows through a webhook payload.
  • Generates OpenAI embeddings for each service record.
  • Stores service facts in Supabase Vector Store for retrieval.
  • Powers the expanded 10-service Telegram answer menu.

Governance Controls

Controlled Sales Alerts

Qualified leads trigger Gmail sales alerts only after n8n validates registration, intent, the score threshold, and the sales handoff gate.

Structured Output

The bot returns JSON so n8n can parse, validate, route, alert, and recover from malformed output.

Public Sanitization

The published workflows show webhook entry points while removing tokens, private URLs, private IDs, and account-specific credentials.
